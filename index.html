Open projects

Tools for science and education.

Small, local-first apps for researchers, instructors, students, and anyone keeping real academic workflows moving on their own machines.

These are small tools from real lab, classroom, writing, scheduling, and home-system headaches. They are built to be useful, easy to test, and respectful of private data. Source links are included when the code is public.

Working rules

Principles

  3. Built for labs, classrooms, and the paperwork around them.
  4. Plain support notes beat polished sales language.
